## 🧭 About Me

I am a Computer Science & Engineering student specializing in Big Data Analytics, passionate about transforming raw data into actionable business insights through analytics, machine learning, and visualization.

My expertise lies at the intersection of Data Collection → Data Cleaning → Analysis → Machine Learning → Business Intelligence. Rather than building isolated models, I develop end-to-end data solutions—from preparing and analyzing complex datasets to creating predictive models, interactive dashboards, and data-driven decision support systems that solve real-world business problems.

* 🚢 **Currently Engineering:** [AEGIS](#-aegis---smart-maritime-boundary-detection-system), a predictive, self-healing maritime safety ecosystem.
* ⚡ **Embedded Focus:** RTOS, ESP32, LoRa Mesh, GPS, and Sensor Fusion.
* 🌐 **Software Focus:** React, Node.js, C++ , PYTHON and Distributed Systems.
* 🧠 **Philosophy:** Solve real-world constraints with engineering rigor. Build. Validate. Optimize. Repeat.
